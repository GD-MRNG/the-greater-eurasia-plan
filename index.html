How it gets done

  1. Go country by country. Each country is its own win, even if an arc takes several trips.
  2. Follow the trunk, take the branches you want. The trunk of each arc is the spine. Branches are optional side trips that rejoin or end on their own.
  3. Group countries into corridors. A run of countries in one trip, sharing one visa, SIM, payment and insurance setup.
  4. Use hubs as bases. Istanbul, Dubai, Tashkent and Bangkok, easy to fly home from and restart from.
  5. Log every trip. Places, dates and a few photos in Notion and Google Maps, so the map keeps growing.

Practical notes

Trip discovery: the first five things to check when working out whether a trip is feasible.

  1. Check entry rules: visa, passport validity, and any onward-ticket or border requirements.
  2. Read the current news and official travel advice to judge whether the trip is feasible now.
  3. Sort out insurance that covers the place and what you plan to do there.
  4. Confirm a payment method and a phone connection (SIM or eSIM) that work there.
  5. Note a hub you can fly from, ideally within a day's travel.
